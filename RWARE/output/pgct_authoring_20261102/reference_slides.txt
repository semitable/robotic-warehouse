Simulation Results: Setup
▶ Environment: 300m×300mmission
area, 8 UAVs, common start base and
final rally point.
▶ Threat scenarios:weakthreat,local D ∗
threat,hard globalthreat
▶ Baselines:
▶ Keep current (ignores the new
threat)
▶ Risk-aware A∗, D ∗ incremental
▶ Edge-safe greedy, Semi-oracle
Evaluation metrics
SG↓overall score (lower is better)
Risk↓accumulated route risk
SkipV↓total value of skipped targets
VCov↑covered mission-value ratio
Wall↓computation (wall-clock) time
▶ Safety requirement: all routes must
satisfyS +(qk)≥S safe (zero unsafe
crossing).

=== SLIDE 13 ===
Simulation Results: Target Classification and Path Re-allocation
▶ Sequential threats appear during the
mission (e.g., att= 25 s, 50 s,
100 s); each degrades the safety map
(red regions).
▶ No-go targets inside the threat
regions (×) are removed from the
active set.
▶ Covered targets (⋆) are reassigned
among the 8 UAVs, which then
move to the final rally point.
▶ Observation: the remaining targets
are covered while every executed
route stays in the safe region.

=== SLIDE 16 ===
Conclusion
▶ Summary: a D ∗-certified, learning-guided mission reallocation framework for
multi-UAV coverage under dynamic threats.
▶ Main contribution: deterministic D ∗ planning as the safety core + learned
target-level mission values in a unified reallocation objective (route cost,
makespan, workload balance, skip penalty, coverage reward).
▶ Validation: hard path safety (zero unsafe crossing) in all scenarios; in the local D ∗
threat scenario, 8.1% lower SG and 8.9% lower skipped mission value than D ∗
incremental replanning.
▶ Future work: decentralized coordination and real-time validation.
